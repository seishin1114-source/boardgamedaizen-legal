Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 24 October 2026 · 日本語

Miyajin Product ("we") sets out below how information is handled in the iOS app "Board Games: Classic Collection" (the "App").

1. Information we collect

  1. Gameplay: your moves and results are processed on your device only. They are not sent to or stored on our servers. The App has no account registration, and never asks you for your name, email address or any other personal information.
  2. Data stored on your device: records of finished games (which game, date and time, duration, number of moves, score, result, AI strength, the sequence of moves and similar; used for statistics and achievements), games in progress, best scores, achievements, whether you have viewed How to Play, your settings (opponent, AI strength, rules, appearance, vibration and similar), a local copy of your purchase status, which games you have unlocked with unlock tickets, and the number of finished games and app launches used to decide how often ads are shown. All of this is stored on your device only.
  3. Purchases: payments for in-app purchases are handled by Apple. We never receive your credit card or other payment details, or your Apple ID. The App only checks which items you own through Apple's StoreKit.
  4. Crash information: the App contains no analytics tools. If you have chosen to share analytics with Apple, we may use the crash and similar information Apple provides to fix problems.
  5. Advertising identifiers: for advertising purposes, advertising providers may collect the IDFA (advertising identifier) and information about your device and usage (except for users who have purchased Plus or Premium; see section 5).

2. How we use it

Information is used to (a) provide the App's features (playing games, keeping your records, statistics, achievements and settings, and providing what you have purchased), (b) fix problems and improve quality, and (c) deliver, optimise and measure advertising.

3. Where data is stored

Game records, settings, unlock status and similar data are stored on your device. They are not sent to or stored on our servers, and they are not synced between devices through iCloud or any other service.

4. In-app purchases

  1. The App offers one-time (non-consumable) in-app purchases: "Plus", "Premium" and "Upgrade to Premium". Purchasing removes ads and lets you play paid games.
  2. Payments and refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's terms. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so after reinstalling the App or on another device you can get them back with "Restore purchases" on the Settings screen.

5. Advertising and tracking (ATT)

  1. The App shows full-screen ads through Google AdMob: an ad (which may be a video) after a game ends, every few games; an ad when you open the App, once every few launches; and video ads that you watch only when you choose to (to play a paid game once, or to get extra undos in 2048). There are no banner ads that stay on screen.
  2. If you have purchased Plus or Premium, no ads are shown and the advertising SDK is not started. In that case the consent form and the tracking permission prompt described below are not shown either.
  3. If you allow tracking in the iOS App Tracking Transparency prompt, the advertising identifier (IDFA) may be used to personalise ads. If you do not allow it, you will see non-personalised ads and the App works exactly the same.
  4. Mechanisms such as SKAdNetwork may be used to measure advertising performance.
  5. In the EEA, the UK and other applicable regions, a consent form provided by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is shown when you first open the App. You can change your choice at any time from "Ad privacy settings" on the App's Settings screen.

6. Sharing with third parties

  1. Except where required by law, we do not sell or provide the information we collect to third parties.
  2. For advertising, advertising identifiers and similar information may be sent to advertising providers such as Google AdMob. Those providers' own privacy policies apply.

7. Third-party services we use

  • Apple (App Store, in-app purchases and related services)
  • Google AdMob (advertising) and Google User Messaging Platform (consent management)

Please refer to each provider's own policy for details (Google advertising policy · Google privacy policy · Apple privacy policy).

8. Retention and deletion

Data held on your device (game records, settings, games unlocked with tickets and similar) is erased when you delete the App. Your purchases remain with your Apple ID and can be restored with "Restore purchases". You can reach us through this contact form.

9. International transfers

Because of the third-party services above, information may be processed outside Japan.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we handle against loss, damage and unauthorised disclosure.

11. Children's privacy

The App is not primarily directed at children under 13.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as needed. We will give notice of significant changes in the App or on this site.

13. Contact

For questions about this policy, please use our contact form.
